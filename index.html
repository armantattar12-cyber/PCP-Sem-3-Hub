How to use this site

A simple study loop instead of rereading slides.

01 • Learn

Flip through active-recall decks, mark Know it vs Still learning, and loop your weak cards.

02 • Recall

Close the explanation and answer the check-yourself questions from memory.

03 • Test

Choose a class, week, material, and quiz length — then review only what you miss.

04 • Return

Go back only to what you missed, then repeat until it feels automatic.
