Pediatric energy: >24 hr to <8 yr → initial 2 J/kg, subsequent 4 J/kg, 2-minute intervals. >8 yr → per RBHP/manufacturer in your course material.

Medical TOR

All conditions: age >16 • arrest not witnessed by paramedic • no ROSC after 20 minutes of resuscitation • no defibrillation delivered.
Contraindications: pregnancy presumed >20 weeks • suspected hypothermia • airway obstruction • non-opioid overdose/toxicology.

DSED / VCD

Age >18 • non-traumatic VF/pulseless VT of presumed cardiac origin • after 3 consecutive standard shocks by paramedics or fire services.

Epinephrine item from your course review

Non-traumatic cardiac arrest • age >24 hr • anaphylaxis suspected as causative event • contraindication: allergy/sensitivity to epinephrine.

Supraglottic Airway

Indication: need for ventilatory assistance or airway control AND other airway management is ineffective.

Condition: absent gag reflex.

Contraindications: foreign-body airway obstruction • known esophageal disease/varices • trauma to the oropharynx • caustic ingestion.
