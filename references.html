HOW TO USE THE FOLDER

Lecture → Standard → Companion

When a lecture teaches a directive, learn the concept from the lecture first. Then verify the exact directive in the ALS PCS and use the Companion Document for clarifications and considerations. Use the BLS PCS whenever the topic is controlled by a BLS standard.
