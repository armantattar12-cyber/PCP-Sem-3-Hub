Evidence-Based Medicine

Core idea: good clinical decisions combine the best available research evidence with clinical expertise and patient preferences. One study alone is not the entire body of evidence.

EvidenceEBMClinical judgment

Research vs Quality Management

Research aims to create knowledge that can be generalized. Quality management focuses on improving a specific service or situation through QA, QI and program evaluation.

ResearchQA/QIREB

Study Misinterpretation

Watch for correlation being mistaken for causation, confounding, bias, small or under-powered samples, poor study design, falsified data and media spin.

BiasConfoundingCritical appraisal

Know the language around adverse events, system errors, no-fault errors, cognitive errors and negligence, and how reporting near misses supports a just culture.

FAST CLASS CHECK

Be able to answer these before class

Abstract: fast summary of the whole study. Methods: exactly how the study was conducted. Results: what was found. Discussion: what those findings mean and their limitations. Conclusion: the main answer and implications. Peer review: subject experts evaluate a manuscript before publication. PubMed: a major biomedical literature database.
